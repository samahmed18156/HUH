Updating selling prices:

 

Select the Prices tab to update the selling prices.

The option to enter ten different selling prices per stock item is given. This is used in conjunction with the debtors detail record whereby the selling price to be used may be selected.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Step 1: Click on tab labelled Prices.

 

Here it will show you the cost price excluding vat and cost price including vat. Also shows the average cost price and the last cost price of the last 4 sales.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

The option is also given to the user to add a particular stock item on promotion for a specific date range.

 

The promotion radio button if selected will activate the promotion selling price regardless of the promotion start and end date, the stock item will therefore be on a

continuous promotion

 

The promotion price must be entered, when invoicing is done and if the stock item is on promotion the promotion price will be automatically selected if the promotion falls within the date range.
