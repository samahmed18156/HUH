Stock Take Process

Select the Adjustments Menu option on the Menu line. Thereafter select Stock Take.

 

The stock take process must be done in the following sequence to ensure that the stock take is correct.

1. Clear the stock take file.

2. Print the stock take list and physically take stock. It is very important that the sequence that the stock take list is printed the stock take capture must be done.

3. Capture the stock take quantity.

4. Print the variance report.

5. Update SOH from Stock Take.

 

Step 1: Select Clear Stock Take file, this will clear the stock.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 Step 2: Select Stock Take List, this will print the stock take sheets. It is very important to note the printing criteria of the stock take list as the same sequence must be selected when entering the stock take quantity.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Step 3: Select Stock Take Capture, enter the stock take quantity from the stock take sheet.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

When entering the stock take quantity, if the quantity is zero then 0 must be entered in the quantity field.

 

Step 4: To view the stock take or variance select Stock Take Enquiry or Variance Enquiry to view a single stock item.

 

Step 5: Select Print Variance Report for the same sequence as the Stock Take and Stock Capture to view/print  the variance report. This report will print the total variance for the stock take.

 

Step 6: Select Update SOH from Stock Table and then select Update S-O-H from Stock Take. This option will then update the stock table with all the quantities from the stock take.
