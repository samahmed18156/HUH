Adding new Stock items:

 

Step 1: Select the Stock Menu option on the Menu line.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Step 2: Select Maintenance from the menu and then select Stock Maintenance.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Step 3: Select the ADD button to create a new stock item.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Enter the stock code that will uniquely identify that particular stock item, if the stock item has a barcode no enter the barcode no. If a unique stock code name is to be entered the code may contain numerics as well as alpha characters.

 

Enter the Description, of the stock item, thereafter enter the Cost Price and Selling Price. The SOH (Stock On Hand) cannot be updated as this is automatically updated when stock is received or sold.

 

Enter the specific Department the product belongs in eg; Ladies Tops or Toiletries.

Enter the Location where the stock is located eg; Shelf 1 or Rack 15.

Once the basic information for the stock item is entered, this item may be invoiced. If additional stock information is required to be entered then continue by selecting the stock item added.
