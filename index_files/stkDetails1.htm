Editing the Stock details:

 

Step 1: Select the stock item that has been added to add additional details pertaining to the stock item.

Step 2: Select the Details button to add/update the stock details.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Enter all the relevant information needed for the stock item on this screen. Certain fields may be left blank whereas other fields are mandatory.

Barcode No: enter a unique barcode no if the barcode was NOT entered in the code field.

RE-Order Quantity is the quantity of stock that must be ordered if SOH is below the Minimum Quantity. A report may be run at any time that will print all stock items that is below the MINIMUM QUANTITY.

 

WEIGHT is how much the item weights.

AREA is how much space the item takes up.

Qty YTD: is the quantity sold for the year, this column is updated automatically.

Qty MTD: is the quantity sold for the month, this column is updated automatically

Value YTD: is the value of the amount that was sold that year.

Value MTD: is the value of the amount that was sold for the month.

Bulk Qty: is the amount of a particular item that is sold in packs. i.e. if you selling coke in 6packs, you will enter that amount there.

Bulk SP: is the selling price of that bulk item.

Bulk SOH: is the amount of bulk stock u have on hand.

Bulk Loc: is the location of the bulk stock.

DATE: will be left as is.

Unit of sale: you can select either EA (Each) or BL (Bulk).

Agent Comm: is the commission, if any, for the sales   made by the salesperson. There are 10 different commission rates that may be entered per stock item.

Creditor: is from which supplier the stock was purchased.

Department:  is in which particular department the item is in eg: Men’s trousers, groceries etc.

 

TIP:

To enable easy searching, click on the label eg Code or Description to automatically enable the search option by code or description.
