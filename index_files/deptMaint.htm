Adding a new Department:

 

Step 1: Select the Stock Menu option on the Menu line.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Step 2: Select Maintenance from the menu and then select Department Maintenance.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Step 3: Select the ADD button to create a new department.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Enter the department Number: for the new department and thereafter the department name.

 

 

 

 

 

 

As many departments as required may be added.
