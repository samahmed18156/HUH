Creating Debtor’s Post Dated Cheques (PDC’s) :

 

Select the Debtors menu option thereafter select PDC’s and PDC Capture.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

The option is given  to search by Account Num, Name, Telephone or Area

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Enter the amount for the PDC, thereafter enter the date that the PDC is due and PDC no (ref no). Select the Age from which the PDC amount must be deducted. The total for the PDC will be added to the PDC total.
